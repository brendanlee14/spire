Discard Mechanics

Rapidly cycle the entire deck through deliberate discards that generate bonus energy and card draw.

Systemic Function

Hyper-efficient deck cycling and energy manipulation. Generates free card draw and action economy by deliberately discarding cards with beneficial discard triggers.

How It Works

The Discard Mechanics engine maximises the Silent's draw capacity by manipulating the game's discard mechanics. Cards like Acrobatics and Calculated Gamble allow rapid deck cycling. By deliberately discarding specific cards like Tactician (which grants 1 Energy when discarded) and Reflex (which draws 3 cards when discarded), the player triggers secondary effects that generate additional energy and card draw.

This creates a fluid action economy where the player can execute highly extended turns, frequently drawing and playing the entire deck before the adversary can respond. Sneaky Strike becomes a 0-cost attack when played while a card has been discarded this turn, enabling free offensive output during cycling turns. Prepared allows drawing and discarding to filter for the exact card needed.
