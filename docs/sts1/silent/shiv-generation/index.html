Shiv Generation

Flood your hand with zero-cost Shivs boosted by Accuracy for high-frequency burst damage.

Systemic Function

High-frequency burst damage utilising zero-cost tokens. Synergises with trigger-on-attack relics to scale defensively while remaining entirely on the offensive.

How It Works

The Shiv Generation engine focuses on the mass production of zero-cost attack tokens. Cards like Blade Dance and Cloak and Dagger flood the player's hand with Shivs. While individually weak at 4 damage each, they synergise aggressively with the Accuracy power, which provides a flat +4 damage bonus to every Shiv played.

Finisher deals damage multiplied by the number of attacks played this turn, making it a powerful finisher after a volley of Shivs. Envenom applies 1 Poison per attack, allowing the Shiv deck to also function as a Poison enabler. The Shuriken relic provides 1 Strength after playing three attacks in a turn, and the Kunai relic provides 1 Dexterity — both trigger repeatedly during a large Shiv volley.
