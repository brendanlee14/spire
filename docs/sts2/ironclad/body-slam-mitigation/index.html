Body Slam Mitigation

Anchor Block permanently with Barricade, stack massive pools via Entrench and Unmovable, then convert them into a single devastating Body Slam.

Systemic Function

Converts permanently-held Block into offensive output. Barricade stops Block from expiring at turn start, allowing multi-turn accumulation. Entrench doubles the pool each activation. Body Slam converts the entire stack into a single devastating attack — defence and offence are the same resource.

How It Works

Barricade (Rare Power, 3→2 Energy) is the foundation of this archetype: while it is in play, Block no longer expires at the start of your turn. This allows the player to accumulate Block across multiple turns rather than regenerating it each cycle, creating an ever-growing pool.

Once Barricade is active, Entrench (Event Skill, 2→1 Energy) doubles the current Block total in a single play — a 50-Block pool becomes 100. Shrug It Off (1 Energy: 8 Block + draw 1), Blood Wall (2 Energy: 16 Block, lose 2 HP), and Flame Barrier (2 Energy: 12 Block plus 4 retaliatory damage per attack received) are the primary feeders. Anchor provides 10 free Block at combat start to seed the pool before any card is played.

Unmovable (Rare Power, 2→1 Energy) doubles the first Block gained from a card each turn — making every Block play 2× more efficient once active. Combined with Barricade, a single Shrug It Off becomes 16 permanent Block.

Impervious (2 Energy: 30 Block, Exhaust) provides a large one-time burst for telegraphed heavy-attack turns. Horn Cleat triggers automatically at the start of turn 2 in every combat for a free 14 Block injection. Body Slam (1→0 Energy) converts the entire Block pool into a single hit — the deck's only required win condition.

↑ Strengths

  • ✓Barricade makes Block permanent — accumulated resources compound across turns rather than resetting each cycle
  • ✓Body Slam provides a single-card kill condition that scales with every Block source simultaneously
  • ✓Consistent across all enemy types regardless of debuff immunity

↓ Weaknesses

  • ✗Requires Barricade to reach peak efficiency — without it, Block resets each turn and Body Slam damage is limited to single-turn generation
  • ✗Enemies that strip Block negate the entire strategy
  • ✗Slower kills than Vulnerable Exploitation against bosses
