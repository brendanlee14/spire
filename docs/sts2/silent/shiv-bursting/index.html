Shiv Bursting

Generate masses of zero-cost Shivs via Blade Dance and Infinite Blades, amplify with Accuracy and Wrist Blade, then close with Finisher.

Systemic Function

High-frequency zero-cost attacks that scale with Accuracy and Wrist Blade. Helical Dart converts each Shiv played into temporary Dexterity, generating Block alongside offensive output. Finisher converts total attack count directly into burst damage.

How It Works

Shivs are 0-cost, 4-damage attacks added directly to hand — their individual damage is negligible without scaling. Accuracy and Wrist Blade are mandatory before the engine becomes lethal.

Accuracy (Uncommon Power, 2 Energy) adds a flat +4 damage to all Shivs per copy in play. Two copies adds +8, three adds +12 — it is a per-copy bonus, not a stacking debuff on enemies. Wrist Blade (Relic) adds +4 damage to all 0-cost attacks for an additional flat multiplier on top of Accuracy.

Blade Dance (Uncommon Skill, 1 Energy: add 3 Shivs — 4 upgraded — to hand, Exhaust) is the primary burst generator. The Exhaust means it naturally removes itself from the deck after use, thinning the draw pile. Cloak and Dagger (Common Skill, 1 Energy: gain 6 Block, add 1 Shiv — 2 Shivs upgraded) provides defensive value alongside Shiv generation.

Infinite Blades (Uncommon Power, 1 Energy) adds 1 Shiv to hand at the start of every turn — the passive per-turn income engine that ensures Shivs are always available regardless of draw. Finisher (Uncommon Attack, 1 Energy: deal 6 damage — 8 upgraded — for each Attack played this turn) converts a Shiv volley into a single concentrated burst.

Helical Dart (Relic) grants 1 Dexterity each time a Shiv is played for the rest of that turn — a volley of 6 Shivs gives 6 Dexterity, translating into 6 Block per future card that generates Block. Ninja Scroll provides 3 Shivs at the start of each combat to seed the opening hand.
