Sly Action Economy

Play discard activators like Acrobatics and Survivor to fire Sly cards for free — Tactician returns Energy, Reflex returns draws, and Flick-Flack fires AoE without spending a single Energy.

Systemic Function

Cheats energy costs by discarding high-value Sly cards, which automatically play for free when discarded from hand. Tactician's Sly trigger generates net-positive energy turns. Tingsha and Tough Bandages convert every discard into simultaneous damage and Block, turning the draw-and-discard loop into a complete offensive and defensive engine.

How It Works

Sly is a keyword that means: if this card is discarded from your hand before the end of your turn, it plays its full effect for free. Sly cards typically cost 1–2 energy — their energy cost only matters if played manually; discarding them bypasses that cost entirely.

The engine has two distinct layers. Discard activators are cards that force a discard but do not have Sly themselves: Acrobatics (Uncommon Skill, 1 Energy: draw 3 cards, discard 1), Survivor (Common Skill, 1 Energy: gain 8 Block, discard 1), and Dagger Throw (Common Attack, 1 Energy: deal 9 damage, draw 1, discard 1). Playing any of these discards a card from hand, firing any Sly card that happens to be the one discarded.

The Sly payoffs are: Tactician (Skill with Sly: gain 1 Energy — 2 upgraded — when discarded; the critical energy engine that keeps the cycle running), Reflex (Skill with Sly: draw 2 cards — 3 upgraded — when discarded; the draw engine), Flick-Flack (Skill with Sly: deal 6 damage — 8 upgraded — to ALL enemies when discarded; the AoE damage payoff), Untouchable (Skill with Sly: gain 6 Block — 8 upgraded — when discarded; the defensive payoff), and Ricochet (2E Attack with Sly: deal 3 damage to a random enemy 4 times — 5 upgraded — when discarded; the single-target damage payoff).

Hand Trick (Uncommon Skill, 1 Energy) adds the Sly keyword to one chosen Skill in hand for the rest of the turn, enabling any Skill to become a Sly trigger on demand. Tingsha (3 damage per discard) and Tough Bandages (3 Block per discard) fire on every discard action, making each Sly trigger simultaneously generate offence and defence.

↑ Strengths

  • ✓Sly cards bypass their energy cost entirely — the engine cheats the fundamental resource constraint

  • ✗Requires Sly payoff cards and discard activators in hand simultaneously — both layers must be drawn together
  • ✗Deck must be kept lean; non-Sly cards dilute the probability of firing a Sly trigger each discard
  • ✗Less effective in Act 1 before Sly payoff cards are drafted
